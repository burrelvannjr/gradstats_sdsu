## 📖 Course Description

This seminar builds quantitative literacy and applied statistical skills for graduate students in public and urban affairs. Students will conduct statistical analyses both by hand and using R/RStudio, learning to interpret and communicate findings through the lens of social science research.

The course moves from foundational descriptive statistics through inferential techniques — culminating in regression analysis — with an emphasis on practical application to real datasets.



## 🎯 Course Learning Objectives

By the end of this course, students will be able to:

1. Describe and summarize data using measures of central tendency and variability
2. Apply the normal distribution and z-scores to interpret data
3. Conduct and interpret bivariate hypothesis tests
4. Perform t-tests, ANOVA, chi-square, correlation, and OLS regression
5. Use R/RStudio to carry out statistical analyses on secondary datasets
6. Communicate quantitative findings through written research-style reports

## 📝 Assignments

### Portfolios 1–4 (100 pts — 25 pts each)
Four short statistical projects spaced throughout the semester. In each portfolio, students are provided a dataset and asked to perform specified statistical techniques (by hand and/or in RStudio) and write up their findings in the style of a research report — complete with a title and all group members' names.

Students are **strongly encouraged** to collaborate in groups of up to three (3) and submit one portfolio per group. Portfolios are intentionally designed to serve as a lasting reference resource after the course ends.

### Course Engagement (50 pts)
Active attendance and participation are essential to success in this seminar, as discussions of quantitative methodology depend on in-class engagement.

### Course Prep & Learning Checks (50 pts)
Week 1 setup checks (email policy, Google Docs, R/RStudio, Zotero, profile, video intro, and portfolio group formation) plus a midterm learning check assessing comprehension of bivariate statistical tests.

### Extra Credit (up to 10 pts)
An optional Portfolio #5 may be offered as extra credit. It must be submitted by the stated deadline — no late submissions accepted.
